System of Interest

PANTRI supports restaurant ingredient planning by turning inventory, usage and expiration records into purchasing advice, retaining manager approval authority and exchanging order information with suppliers.

Inside this skeleton: the PANTRI information workflow for UC.1.

External: Restaurant Manager and Ingredient Supplier. Staff-supplied records are preloaded for this use case.

Physical delivery, payment, other use cases and a complete service architecture are outside this implementation slice.
